## What's wrong today (your honest opener material)
1. The site's **headline and footer show a keyword string** ("Roofing garage doors decks home improvement painting drywall…") **instead of "Wallace Roofing."**
2. The "Explore Our Stunning Roofs" gallery has **blank placeholder tiles**. The **"Happy Customers" section is empty**, and "Our History" has a heading but no text, for a 95-year-old company.
3. Headings repeat ("Our Approach", "Our Expertise").
4. **License mismatch:** the site shows **MHIC 1566**, but BBB lists **MHIC 154830**. Homeowners and insurers check this. *Look it up on Maryland Labor's MHIC license search yourself before you mention it.*
5. The old domain wallaceroofingcompany.com (still on YellowPages) no longer resolves.

Credit what works: they *do* have a quote form with file upload, and that's worth keeping.

## Best approach
**Call first, then drop by.** It's a family business with an office on N Rolling Rd. Ask for **Jim Wallace (Operations)**. Mid-morning Tue–Thu is best; avoid rainy days, when they're slammed.

## Likely objections
- **"We already have a website."** "You do, and the quote form is good. It's the first impression that's costing you: the headline, the empty sections. This keeps what works and fixes the rest."
- **"We get our work from referrals."** "After 95 years I'd expect that. Referrals still Google you before they call. This makes sure what they find matches your reputation."
- **"GoDaddy is cheap."** "It is. We can host for $99/mo with edits included, or hand you the files."

## Before the walkthrough, ask for
Real project photos (slate jobs especially), logo files, the current MHIC number, which phone line to feature, the service area, any warranty or financing they actually offer, and permission to quote reviews (Angi/Google).
