## The opener
They have a **near-perfect reputation and no website**. They even bought the domain and renewed it in June 2026, but it just bounces to Facebook. Customers searching "auto repair Windsor Mill" can't see the services, can't book, and many can't get past Facebook's login.

## Best approach
**Walk in.** You're a neighbor on Windsor Mill Rd. Go mid-morning Tue–Thu, and avoid 8–9am drop-off and 4:30–5pm pickup. Bring the demo on your phone *and* a tablet. Keep it under 3 minutes; they're working.

> "Hey, I'm ___, I do websites for businesses around here, I'm just up the road in Windsor Mill. Quick thing: you've got a 4.9 from 65 reviews on Google, which is awesome, but tiercarcare.com just sends people to Facebook, and a lot of them can't get past the login. I built you a sample of what your own site could look like, with appointment requests built in. Can I show you real quick?"

*(Hand them the phone. Let them scroll. Stop talking.)*

> "Since you already own tiercarcare.com, I can point it at this and have it live in about a week. If you don't want it, no problem. Here's my card with the link."

## Likely objections
- **"We're busy enough."** "Good problem. The appointment form lets you see the car and the problem *before* they show up, and fills the slow days. It also keeps you from losing the customers who won't call."
- **"Facebook works fine."** "Keep it. But Google ranks websites, not Facebook pages, and a lot of people can't see your page without logging in."
- **"How much?"** "$1,800 to build, since you already own the domain, and $99 a month for hosting and any changes you want. One extra brake job a month covers it."

## Before launch, ask for
The services they actually offer and want to promote, any certifications (ASE, etc.), warranty terms, real shop photos, their logo, permission to quote Google reviews, and access to tiercarcare.com DNS at DreamHost.
