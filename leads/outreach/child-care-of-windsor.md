## What's wrong today
1. **Their YellowPages/Superpages listing sends parents to a used-car dealership in Illinois** (windsorautoinc.com). This is the strongest, most sympathetic opener.
2. The site's **email link is broken** (`mailto:---`). Parents literally can't email them from the site.
3. **Analytics have been dead since 2023**: it still uses Universal Analytics, which Google shut down.
4. There's **no way to request a tour or join a waitlist** online. Enrollment paperwork is PDF downloads, and one is misspelled ("medicaion-administration.pdf").
5. The "Tuition" section lists payment methods only. That's fine, but it should invite a call or a tour.

## Best approach: be respectful, it's a childcare center
**Call first. Don't walk in unannounced** (centers are rightly wary of strangers). Ask for **Ms. Roche**. Call mid-day (around 12:30–2pm, nap time), never during drop-off (7–9am) or pickup (4–6pm). Their current site was built by another designer, so don't knock it. Focus on the listing error and on what parents can't do.

### Phone
> "Hi, may I speak with Ms. Roche? … Ms. Roche, my name is ___. I run a small web design studio here in Windsor Mill. I'm calling because I noticed something you'd want to know about: your YellowPages listing sends parents to a car dealership's website in Illinois. I can show you how to fix that for free. I also put together a sample of what a new website for Child Care of Windsor could look like, where parents can book a tour or join your waitlist from their phone. Would it be all right if I emailed you the link?"

## Likely objections
- **"We're full / we have a waitlist."** "Then the online waitlist saves your staff the phone time, and keeps families warm until a spot opens."
- **"Our designer handles the website."** "Of course. I'm happy to share the design with them, or to just fix the listing problem."
- **"Parents find us by word of mouth."** "They do. Then they look you up. Right now some of them land on a car lot."

## Important for launch
Enrollment forms collect **sensitive child health and family information**. Don't email those forms around. Use an encrypted form provider (with a signed data-processing agreement) or keep paper forms in the center, and link to the Maryland OCC requirements. Get written permission before using any photos of children.
